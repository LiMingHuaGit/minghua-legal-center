Product Support产品支持

Start with the questions below. If the issue remains, email the developer with reproducible details.请先查看下列常见问题；如果问题仍未解决，请通过邮件提供可复现的信息。

Common questions常见问题

How do I start?如何开始使用？

Open excalidraw.com, open the extension side panel, and choose a local folder.

打开 excalidraw.com，开启扩展侧边栏，并选择本地目录。

Why does the folder need permission again?为什么需要重新授权目录？

Chrome may require permission again after a restart or reset. Use Reconnect.

Chrome 可能在重启或权限重置后要求再次授权，请使用“重新授权”。

Why was a canvas not saved?为什么画布没有保存？

Keep the Excalidraw tab open, confirm folder write permission, and try a manual save.

保持 Excalidraw 标签页打开，确认目录写入权限，并尝试手动保存。

Which files are supported?支持哪些文件？

The workspace scans .excalidraw files.

工作区扫描 .excalidraw 文件。

How do I remove local data?如何移除本地数据？

Delete workspace files, revoke folder access, clear extension storage, or uninstall.

删除工作区文件、撤销目录权限、清理扩展存储或卸载扩展。

Still need help?仍需帮助？

Include the product name, version, device and operating-system version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce. Remove passwords, payment details, private content or personal records, and unrelated personal information from screenshots or attachments.请注明产品名称、版本、设备与操作系统版本、预期结果、实际现象及复现步骤。发送截图或附件前，请移除密码、付款信息、私密内容或个人记录及无关个人信息。
