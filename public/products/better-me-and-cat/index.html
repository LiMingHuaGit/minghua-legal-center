Actual data boundaries实际数据边界

On your device设备本地

Todos, habits, focus records, cat state, balances, settings, anonymous Screen Time allowlist tokens, and the limited snapshot shared locally with the app's own widgets through App Group.待办、习惯、专注记录、猫咪状态、余额、设置、匿名屏幕使用时间白名单标识，以及通过 App Group 在设备上与自有小组件共享的有限快照。

Apple platform controlsApple 平台控制

Apple processes StoreKit account, payment, and transaction information. iOS controls Screen Time authorization, notifications, and Live Activities. App data may enter an iCloud or device backup only when your backup settings allow it.Apple 处理 StoreKit 账户、付款与交易信息；iOS 管理屏幕使用时间授权、通知与灵动岛。仅当您的备份设置允许时，App 数据才可能进入 iCloud 或设备备份。

Developer service开发者服务

No sign-in, developer backend, advertising, analytics, tracking, camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.不登录、不使用开发者后端、广告、分析或追踪，也不访问相机、麦克风、通讯录或位置。
