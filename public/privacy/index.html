PushUp Pilot — product disclosure

Purpose and account model

PushUp Pilot is a camera-controlled fitness arcade game. It is not a medical device, health-measurement service, or substitute for professional advice. The app has local player profiles but does not create an authenticated online account.

Information processed on the iPhone

  • Live camera frames and Vision body-pose landmarks used for motion control and calibration.
  • Calibration profiles, control preferences, language, theme, character, and recording settings.
  • Local player names and avatar images.
  • Local record snapshots, including player name and avatar, score, completed push-up count, duration, clap push-up count, record category, and date where available.
  • Pending upload state and the app's weekly allowance status.
  • Optional ReplayKit videos. Recording is off by default, excludes microphone audio, and saves completed recordings to Photos after permission is granted.
  • Analytics report images that you explicitly choose to save to Photos.

Deleting a local player removes that profile and calibration data, but prior local record snapshots retain the name and avatar until you also clear Records. Deleting the app removes ordinary local app data subject to iOS backup behavior. Media already saved to Photos must be deleted in Photos.

PushUp Pilot does not use HealthKit and does not upload camera frames, pose landmarks, local names, local avatars, replay videos, or report images to its Cloudflare service.

Global leaderboard

Creating a local player registers an app-generated random UUID with the Cloudflare service. The service creates a non-editable public alias: Pilot- followed by the UUID without hyphens. Opening the leaderboard sends the selected player UUID as a query parameter.

Eligible uploads include the player UUID, a random challenge UUID, numeric score, server-recorded time, ranking and challenge-count data, and weekly quota state. Public entries show the automatic alias, score, rank, challenge count, and update time. Local names and avatars are not uploaded or shown.

Free users can manually complete one successful eligible upload per player UUID per UTC week, beginning Monday at 00:00 UTC. Failed, rejected, duplicate, or rolled-back attempts do not consume the allowance. Lifetime Pro queues eligible standard scores for automatic upload. Manual-control scores and scores with detected knee support are entertainment records and are not eligible.

The service accepts no public profile text, user-selected public nicknames, images, audio, comments, messages, chat, or posts. Content reporting and user blocking therefore do not apply to this leaderboard.

StoreKit and Game Center

Lifetime Pro is an optional one-time, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase. For premium uploads, the app sends Apple's signed transaction JWS to the Cloudflare Worker. The Worker transiently decodes and verifies the bundle ID, product ID, environment, transaction identifier, purchase and revocation metadata, and entitlement status. The raw JWS is not intentionally persisted in D1 or application logs.

The app asks Apple Game Center to authenticate at launch. For authenticated users, eligible best scores and milestone achievements are submitted automatically to Apple. Apple manages Game Center account information, display name, records, availability, and privacy settings. Game Center data is separate from the Cloudflare leaderboard.

Providers and retention

D1 application recordsNo automatic expiration. Retained while needed to operate the leaderboard, until the service is retired, or until an applicable, reasonably verified request is completed.
Workers LogsRetained for 3 days under the current Cloudflare Free plan. Logs may contain IP address, request time, path, status, headers or technical details, security signals, and error diagnostics.
D1 Time TravelRecovery history is retained for 7 days under the current Cloudflare Free plan. Deleted or changed rows may remain recoverable during that window.
Apple servicesStoreKit and App Store provide purchase, redemption, and restoration; Game Center provides authentication, leaderboards, and achievements; Vision, AVFoundation, ReplayKit, and Photos provide on-device camera, pose, recording, and saving features.

We do not intentionally place camera content, local profile names or avatars, or raw StoreKit JWS values in Workers Logs.

Data requests

PushUp Pilot has no authenticated server account and no server-side self-service deletion endpoint. Uninstalling the app or deleting a local player does not automatically remove Cloudflare leaderboard rows, Game Center records, Apple purchase history, or media saved to Photos.

Email 2290320152@qq.com with subject PushUp Pilot Data Request, your automatic alias or player UUID, country or region, and request. Because aliases and UUIDs can be public, they alone do not prove control. We will acknowledge the request within 5 business days and explain whether additional limited evidence is reasonably available. We do not promise deletion where control cannot reasonably be verified. Valid requests are handled as required by applicable law, subject to the 7-day D1 Time Travel recovery window.

Use Apple's controls for Game Center and purchase history. Use Photos to delete replay videos or report images.

PushUp Pilot — 产品专项披露

用途与账户模式

PushUp Pilot 是一款通过相机动作控制的健身街机游戏，不属于医疗器械、健康测量服务，也不能替代专业建议。App 包含本地玩家档案，但不会创建经过身份验证的在线账户。

在 iPhone 本地处理的信息

  • 用于动作控制和校准的实时相机画面及 Vision 身体姿态关键点。
  • 校准档案、控制偏好、语言、主题、角色和录像设置。
  • 本地玩家名称和头像图片。
  • 本地记录快照，包括可用情况下的玩家名称与头像、分数、完成俯卧撑次数、持续时间、击掌俯卧撑次数、记录类别和日期。
  • 待上传分数状态和 App 保存的每周免费次数状态。
  • 可选 ReplayKit 回放视频。录像默认关闭、不包含麦克风音频，并在获得权限后将完成录像保存到系统相册。
  • 您主动选择保存到系统相册的训练统计报告图片。

删除本地玩家会移除该档案及校准数据，但以往本地记录中的名称和头像快照会继续保留，直到您同时在“记录”中执行清空。删除 App 会移除一般本地 App 数据，但可能受 iOS 备份机制影响。已经保存到系统相册的媒体需要在“照片”中删除。

PushUp Pilot 不使用 HealthKit，也不会把相机画面、姿态关键点、本地名称、本地头像、回放视频或报告图片上传至其 Cloudflare 服务。

全球排行榜

创建本地玩家时，App 会立即向 Cloudflare 服务登记随机 UUID。服务会生成不可编辑的公开别名：Pilot- 加去掉连字符的 UUID。打开排行榜时，App 会把所选玩家 UUID 作为查询参数发送。

上传符合条件的分数时，服务处理玩家 UUID、随机挑战 UUID、数字分数、服务端记录时间、排名与挑战次数，以及每周额度状态。公开内容显示自动别名、分数、排名、挑战次数和更新时间；本地名称与头像不会上传或显示。

免费用户每个玩家 UUID 每个 UTC 周可手动成功上传一次，周期从周一 00:00 UTC 开始。失败、被拒绝、重复或回滚的请求不会消耗次数。Lifetime Pro 会把符合条件的标准分数加入自动上传队列。使用手动控制或检测到膝盖支撑的分数属于娱乐记录，不具备全球排行榜资格。

服务不接受公开个人简介、用户自选公开昵称、图片、音频、评论、消息、聊天或帖子，因此内容举报和用户屏蔽不适用。

StoreKit 与 Game Center

Lifetime Pro 是可选的一次性非消耗型 Apple App 内购买。进行高级版上传时，App 会将 Apple 签名的交易 JWS 发送到 Cloudflare Worker。Worker 临时解码并验证 Bundle ID、产品 ID、环境、交易标识符、购买与撤销元数据及权益状态。原始 JWS 不会被有意持久化到 D1 或应用日志中。

App 启动时会请求 Apple Game Center 进行身份验证。已登录用户的符合条件最佳分数和里程碑成就会自动提交给 Apple。Game Center 账户信息、显示名称、记录、可用性和隐私设置均由 Apple 管理，与 Cloudflare 排行榜相互独立。

服务商与保留期

D1 业务记录目前没有自动过期时间。在排行榜运营所需期间保留，或在服务停止后、适用且可合理验证的请求完成后处理。
Workers Logs当前 Cloudflare Free 套餐下保留 3 天。日志可能包含 IP 地址、请求时间、路径、状态、请求头或其他技术细节、安全信号和错误诊断。
D1 Time Travel当前 Cloudflare Free 套餐下恢复历史保留 7 天。已删除或修改的数据行在该窗口内可能仍可恢复。
Apple 服务StoreKit 与 App Store 提供购买、兑换和恢复；Game Center 提供身份验证、排行榜和成就；Vision、AVFoundation、ReplayKit 与 Photos 提供设备端相机、姿态、录制和保存功能。

我们不会有意把相机内容、本地玩家名称或头像、原始 StoreKit JWS 写入 Workers Logs。

数据请求

PushUp Pilot 不存在经过身份验证的服务端账户，也没有服务端自助删除接口。卸载 App 或删除本地玩家不会自动移除 Cloudflare 排行榜数据、Game Center 记录、Apple 购买历史或已保存到系统相册的媒体。

请发送邮件至 2290320152@qq.com，主题写明 PushUp Pilot Data Request，并提供自动别名或玩家 UUID、所在国家或地区及具体请求。由于别名和 UUID 可能公开可见，这些信息本身不能证明控制权。我们将在 5 个工作日内确认收到请求，并说明是否存在可合理提供的其他有限证明。在无法合理验证控制权时，我们不会承诺删除。有效请求将依适用法律处理，并受 D1 Time Travel 7 天恢复窗口影响。

Game Center 和购买历史相关请求请使用 Apple 的控制工具；回放视频或报告图片请在“照片”中删除。
