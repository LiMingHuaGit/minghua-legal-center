1. Scope and product policies

This policy states the baseline privacy principles for products and services published by MingHua Li. Every product has a separate policy describing its actual data types, permissions, providers, retention, and deletion choices.

Product-specific terms control. If a product policy differs from this general policy, the product policy controls for that product.

2. General principles

Products process only information reasonably needed for requested features, purchases, security, support, and legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Some products are entirely local. Others use named platform or hosting services for specific optional or online features. The applicable product policy identifies those boundaries.

3. Information that may be involved

  • Content and settings you create inside a product.
  • App-generated identifiers or records required for a requested feature.
  • Purchase and entitlement information processed by an app-store platform.
  • Support email content and attachments you voluntarily provide.
  • Limited network and security information processed by a hosting provider when a product uses an online service.

Sensitive device permissions are requested only when a feature needs them and the operating system presents the request.

4. Providers and processing regions

Depending on the product, providers may include Apple, Google Chrome, Cloudflare, Excalidraw, and an email provider. Providers receive only the information required for their role and operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

Providers may process information outside your country or region. Product availability, currency, platform services, and privacy rights may vary by location.

5. Retention, security, and rights

Information is kept only while reasonably needed for the stated purpose, while a service remains active, while a request is handled, or as required by law. Exact periods and deletion behavior appear in each product policy.

We use reasonable safeguards such as HTTPS for online services, limited data fields, platform permission controls, and restricted credentials. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Requests may be limited when control cannot reasonably be verified, another person's rights would be affected, or retention is required by law.

6. Changes and contact

We may update these terms when products, providers, laws, or data practices change. Product pages show their own effective dates.

1. 适用范围与产品政策

本政策说明 MingHua Li 发布的产品与服务所遵循的通用隐私原则。每款产品都有独立政策，具体说明实际涉及的数据类型、权限、服务商、保留与删除方式。

产品专项政策优先。如果产品专项政策与本通用政策存在差异，以该产品的专项政策为准。

2. 通用原则

产品仅处理为提供用户请求的功能、完成购买、保护服务、响应支持及履行法律义务而合理必要的信息。我们不会出售或出租个人信息，也不会将其用于跨 App 或跨网站广告追踪。

部分产品完全在本地运行；部分产品会为明确的可选或在线功能使用已列明的平台或托管服务。具体边界以相应产品政策为准。

3. 可能涉及的信息

  • 您在产品内创建的内容与设置。
  • 为实现所请求功能而生成的标识符或记录。
  • 由应用商店平台处理的购买与权益信息。
  • 您主动发送的支持邮件正文与附件。
  • 产品使用在线服务时，由托管服务商处理的有限网络与安全信息。

仅在产品功能确有需要且操作系统展示授权请求时使用敏感设备权限。

4. 服务商与处理地区

根据具体产品，服务商可能包括 Apple、Google Chrome、Cloudflare、Excalidraw 与邮箱服务商。服务商仅接收完成其职责所需的信息，并依据自身条款与隐私说明处理。

服务商可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息。产品可用性、货币、平台服务和隐私权利可能因地区而异。

5. 保留、安全与权利

信息仅在实现所述目的、相关服务仍在运行、请求正在处理或法律要求的期间保留。具体期限和删除行为请查看各产品政策。

在线服务使用 HTTPS、有限数据字段、平台权限控制和受限凭据等合理保护措施。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

根据您所在地区，您可能有权请求访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、数据可携带或撤回同意。如无法合理验证控制权、请求会影响他人权利或法律要求继续保留，请求可能受到限制。

6. 更新与联系

产品、服务商、法律或数据实践发生变化时，我们可能更新相关条款。各产品页面会显示自己的生效日期。
